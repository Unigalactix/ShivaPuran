सहचर-संहिताः · Companion Codices

The Sacred Codices

Four sanctums on one shelf — the epics and the Purāṇas retold as living codices, their key scenes painted in the Madhubani folk idiom. Walk them in any order; every door opens on the same devotion in a different key.
